SPECTRA FPO & OFPO Website – Detailed Project Prompt

Project Overview

Design and develop a modern, premium, responsive website for SPECTRA, an organization working with both Farmer Producer Organizations (FPO) and Other Farmer Producer Organizations (OFPO).

The website is not a management portal and not an ERP.

Its primary purpose is to:

Present the SPECTRA brand professionally.

Showcase FPO and OFPO initiatives.

Display products beautifully.

Build trust among customers, partners, and organizations.

Allow visitors to browse products and send enquiries.

Highlight the social impact of supporting farmers and artisans.

The overall experience should feel like a premium brand website rather than a government website.



Website Goals

The website should:

Represent SPECTRA professionally.

Increase trust and credibility.

Showcase both business divisions equally.

Display products with high-quality visuals.

Tell the story behind every product.

Encourage customer enquiries.

Be mobile-friendly.

Be SEO-friendly.

Be accessible and fast.
